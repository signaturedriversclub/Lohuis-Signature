Questions

01

How far ahead should we ask?

A day is often enough for a transfer. Multi-day assignments, journeys and anything involving a particular car are better placed weeks ahead — we hold a limited number of commissions at a time.

02

Where do you work?

From Aalsmeer across the Netherlands, and by road throughout continental Europe. Journeys regularly run into Belgium, Germany, France, Switzerland and Italy.

03

Can you drive our own car?

Yes. Our chauffeurs are used to collection and marque cars, and the vehicle is returned exactly as it left. Handover is arranged at the residence or office.

04

How is a commission priced?

Against the day rather than the distance. Vehicle, chauffeur, timings and cost arrive in one written proposal, amended until it is right. Nothing is added afterwards without agreement.

05

Will it be the same chauffeur?

Within a commission, yes. Across a retainer, the same named chauffeur is held against your diary, with one familiar alternate briefed behind them.

06

How discreet is discreet?

Names, routes and requests stay inside the house. We do not publish client lists, photograph passengers, or discuss one commission with another.

07

What if plans change on the day?

Late changes are expected rather than penalised. One number reaches someone who knows your name and the brief, and the day is re-cut around you.

08

What can we ask the concierge for?

Rooms and suites, tables, boxes and private views, guests met and looked after, and the requests that fit no category. If it sits beyond us we say so plainly and point you somewhere better.

09

Do we book the hotels ourselves?

You may, but most clients let us. Where we reserve on your behalf we act as agent: the house or restaurant contracts with you, and their cancellation terms apply alongside ours.

10

Is a journey ever shared with others?

Never. A journey is written for one party. Shared drives for a circle of motoring enthusiasts are the business of our sibling house, Signature Drivers Club.

11

What if weather closes a route?

Every journey is written with alternatives already reconnoitred for weather, closures and mood. The day is re-cut without a conversation about surcharges.

Privacy

Discretion is the service. This notice sets out what that means for your data.

Who we are

Lohuis Signature, Twijnderlaan 19, 1431 DD Aalsmeer, Netherlands. KvK 76937690, VAT NL00460544B82. We are the controller of the personal data described here. Write to info@lohuissignature.nl on any question in this notice.

What we hold

Only what a commission requires: your name and contact details, the party travelling, dates, addresses and timings, billing details, and any preference or sensitivity you choose to tell us. We do not build profiles and we do not buy data.

Why we hold it

To answer an enquiry and to perform the commission, to meet tax and administrative obligations, and to run the house itself. Where we rely on legitimate interest, it is the plain interest of arranging travel that works.

Who sees it

The people arranging your commission, and the chauffeur assigned to it — no more than the day requires. Hotels, restaurants and partner operators receive only the detail needed to hold a booking. Nothing is sold, listed or passed on for marketing.

How long

Enquiries that come to nothing are removed within twelve months. Records tied to invoices are kept for seven years, as Dutch law requires. Retainer clients may ask us to keep standing preferences on file, and may ask us to forget them at any time.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask for a copy of what we hold, ask us to correct or erase it, object to a use, or ask for it in portable form. Write to us and you will have an answer within a month. You may also complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Concierge and journeys

Where we reserve a room, a table or an event on your behalf, we pass only the names and details that booking requires — and only to that house. Journeys across Europe mean the same detail may reach hotels and partner operators in other EU or EEA countries; where a route takes us beyond them, we share nothing without telling you first. Dietary notes, access needs or anything else you tell us in confidence are given to a venue only with your say-so, and are never kept longer than the commission.

This website

The site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. Enquiry details reach us by email and are held as described above.

Terms

Every commission is written individually. These terms sit behind that proposal, not in front of it.

The agreement

A commission begins when you accept a written proposal from Lohuis Signature. That proposal — vehicle, chauffeur, timings and cost — governs the work. Where it differs from these terms, the proposal prevails.

Rates and payment

Rates are quoted per commission and exclude VAT unless stated. Waiting time beyond the agreed allowance, additional stops, tolls and parking are charged at cost. Invoices are payable within fourteen days; retainers are invoiced monthly in advance.

Changes and cancellation

Timings may be moved at no cost wherever we can absorb them. Transfers cancelled within twenty-four hours of the agreed pick-up, and multi-day commissions cancelled within seven days of departure, are charged in full unless we agree otherwise. Third-party bookings made on your behalf follow that supplier's own terms.

Vehicles and chauffeurs

We reserve the right to substitute a vehicle of equal or better standard where necessary. Chauffeurs observe traffic law, driving-hours rules and their own judgement on road and weather conditions; those judgements are final. Smoking is not permitted in our vehicles.

Your own vehicle

Where we drive a car you own, it must be roadworthy, taxed and insured for a third-party driver. Please tell us of any condition, modification or handling matter before the day.

Concierge arrangements

Where we reserve accommodation, tables, tickets or access on your behalf, we act as your agent. The contract is between you and that house, and its own terms, deposits and cancellation windows apply in addition to ours. Sums we pay out on your behalf are recharged at cost; a handling fee, where one applies, is stated in the proposal.

Journeys

A journey is written for one party and priced as a whole. Routes are reconnoitred and alternatives held in reserve; we may re-cut a day for weather, closure, road conditions or safety without that being a change to the commission. Where a journey depends on third-party suppliers, their performance is theirs — we will always press your case, but we do not underwrite it. Journeys cancelled within seven days of departure are charged in full, and any non-refundable sums already committed on your behalf remain payable.

Liability

We carry the insurance required for passenger transport in the Netherlands. We are not liable for delay or loss caused by matters outside our control — traffic, weather, closures, industrial action or the acts of third parties. Personal effects remain your responsibility. Nothing here limits liability for death, personal injury or intent.

Governing law

Dutch law applies. Should something go wrong, tell us first — most matters are settled by conversation. Failing that, disputes fall to the competent court in Amsterdam.
