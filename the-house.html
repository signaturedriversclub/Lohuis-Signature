The House

Small on purpose, and built around one standard rather than a fleet size.

We grew out of moving people who could not afford a surprise.

Corporate boards, visiting delegations, families with a schedule that could not slip. That work taught us to prepare more than we are asked to and to say less about it afterwards.

We stayed small deliberately. Every commission is still briefed by someone who knows the client, and the person who writes the proposal is the person you reach on the day.

From Aalsmeer we work across the Netherlands and continental Europe — chauffeured movement, concierge arrangements and private journeys, for a register of clients that grows slowly.
