How a commission is made

Four steps, one signature

No booking engine and no call centre. Every arrangement is written by the person who will answer for it.

01

The conversation

Dates, party, sensitivities. Ten minutes on the telephone tells us more than any form.

02

The written proposal

Vehicle, chauffeur, timings and cost in one document. Amended until it is right.

03

The preparation

Cars presented the night before. Routes reconnoitred. Contingencies held in reserve, unmentioned.

04

The day itself

One number to reach, answered by someone who knows your name and the brief.
