Commission No. I · Roadshow

Roadshows

A car and a chauffeur for a roadshow: several meetings in a day, or several cities in a week.

One person at the wheel from the first collection to the last drop at the airport.

The schedule is the difficult part. The driving follows it.

A roadshow is planned against the list of meetings rather than the map. Each leg is timed with room for the meeting that runs over, and the car stays nearby between appointments instead of leaving and coming back.

Across borders

Belgium and Germany are a morning's drive, France and Switzerland a day's. When a week runs abroad, the chauffeur stays close to your hotel and brings the car home at the end where driving hours allow.

Working on the way

Between meetings the car is somewhere to read, prepare and take calls. For a team, some of our V-Classes have a table to work at.

Notice

A roadshow needs more notice than a transfer, and the more cities it covers, the earlier it is worth placing.

One day, many meetings

A team, not one person

Samples and cases

The car

Chosen for the week

An S-Class or 7 Series for one to three people, a V-Class for a team of up to seven, and a Sprinter when the party is larger. All of them are in the fleet.

The arrangement

Written against the schedule

One proposal sets out the car, the chauffeur, each day's timings and the cost, priced against the days rather than the kilometres.

Roadshows are part of The Chauffeur, which also covers transfers, events and delegations.

01

Will it be the same chauffeur for the whole roadshow?

Yes. One chauffeur stays with the commission from the first day to the last, and knows the schedule before it starts.

02

What happens if a meeting runs over?

The car waits. Each leg is timed with room to spare, and the next appointment is re-timed with you if it needs to be.

03

Can the roadshow go abroad?

Yes, by road through Belgium, Germany, France, Switzerland and Italy. On longer trips the chauffeur stays in a hotel nearby.

04

How is a roadshow priced?

By the day rather than the distance. The full cost is in the written proposal before the first day, and nothing is added without your agreement.
