Commission No. II

The Concierge

The arrangements around the movement. Asked once, remembered afterwards.

A private concierge for Amsterdam, and for wherever the itinerary goes next.

A table that was full. A florist at seven in the morning. A suite held while the meeting overran.

The concierge commission exists because the car is rarely the whole request. It is handled by the same people who hold your itinerary, so nothing has to be explained twice.

Most of it happens outside the Netherlands. A suite held in Monte Carlo, a table in Milan, a house arranged before the party lands — wherever the itinerary goes.

The Concierge usually grows out of a chauffeur commission, but it is not tied to one. It can be taken on its own, for a single occasion or held month to month.

One person holds your dossier and stays with it. There are others behind them, but the file does not change hands. They sit with the chauffeurs rather than apart from them, which is why nothing has to be relayed twice. The telephone is answered at any hour — someone is awake, and able to act on it.

Commission No. II · from the gate

It was arranged in thirty minutes.

Accommodation

Rooms and suites in houses we know, held under our name where it helps.

We keep standing relationships with a number of houses — Monte Carlo, Strasbourg and Bormio among them — but we are not limited to them. The right house is the one that suits the party, and we will find it whether or not we have used it before.

Tables & access

Restaurants, boxes, private views and the occasional closed door.

Notice depends entirely on the door. Some want a week; some want an hour. Tell us what you would like and you will get an honest answer about whether it can be done.

Guest hosting

Visiting partners met, moved and looked after on your behalf, to your standard.

If you are flying someone in, the whole of it can sit with us: the ticket or the aircraft, VIP handling or a personal meeting at the aircraft steps, the chauffeur, and whatever else the day turns out to need.

The unlisted

Requests that fit no category. Ask; the answer is usually yes, quietly.

A client once telephoned from the gate, an hour from boarding for Amsterdam, wanting VIP handling and an S-Class for the rest of the day. It was arranged in thirty minutes. Dinner that evening was at a restaurant usually committed weeks in advance.

In Amsterdam

The part that happens at home.

For those arriving, Amsterdam is usually the first thing to be arranged: the meeting at Schiphol, the car, the hotel and a table for the first evening. It can all sit in one request, made once.

For those who live or work here, it is more often about other people. Guests and visiting partners are met, moved and looked after on your behalf, in Amsterdam, on the Zuidas or in The Hague, and to your standard.

The house is in Aalsmeer, minutes from Schiphol, so the person holding your dossier is close to both the airport and the city. Names, addresses and requests stay inside the house.

01

Is this a private concierge service in Amsterdam?

Yes. The house is in Aalsmeer, minutes from Schiphol, and works most often in Amsterdam and The Hague. The same concierge follows the itinerary abroad, so a request that begins in Amsterdam does not change hands when it leaves.

02

What can we ask the concierge for?

Rooms and suites, tables, boxes and private views, guests met and looked after, and the requests that fit no category. If it sits beyond us we say so plainly and point you somewhere better.

03

Do we need a chauffeur commission first?

No. The Concierge usually grows out of a chauffeur commission, but it can be taken on its own, for a single occasion or held month to month.

04

How discreet is the concierge?

Names, routes and requests stay inside the house. We do not publish client lists, photograph passengers, or discuss one commission with another, unless a client has specifically agreed otherwise.

05

At what hours can the concierge be reached?

At any hour. The telephone is answered by someone who is awake and able to act, and one person holds your dossier throughout.
