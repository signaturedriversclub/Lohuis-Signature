Choosing between them

Most requests sit plainly in one commission. Where they do not, the first conversation sorts it out.

A single movement, an airport arrival or a day of meetings is The Chauffeur. Anything around the movement that is not driving, a room, a table, a guest to look after, is The Concierge.

A route of several days, planned and driven before you see it, is The Journey. When the same car and the same chauffeur are wanted week after week, it becomes The Retainer.

They also combine. A chauffeur commission often brings a concierge request with it, and both sit in the same written proposal.

01

How is a commission placed?

With a conversation, by telephone, email or WhatsApp. A written proposal follows with the car, the chauffeur, the timings and the cost, and changes until it is right.

02

Can a commission be for a single day?

Yes. The Chauffeur and The Concierge are taken for one occasion as readily as for longer. The Journey and The Retainer are longer by nature.

03

Do you work outside the Netherlands?

Yes, by road throughout continental Europe. Belgium, Germany, France, Switzerland and Italy are where the work goes most often.

04

Who answers on the day?

The person who wrote the proposal, or someone beside them who knows the brief. There is one number, answered at any hour.
